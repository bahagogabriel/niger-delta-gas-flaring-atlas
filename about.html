ABOUT THE STUDY

Why we made this map.
And what it can tell us.

We studied 14 years of satellite-based estimates to understand where gas flaring has been reported in the Niger Delta. This map helps people see which locations might deserve closer investigation—either to reduce flaring or to plan further checks near communities and mangroves. It does not prove that anyone or any habitat has been harmed.

02 / POSITION

Why we carried out this study

The study evaluates satellite-estimated gas-flaring activity at individual reported sites over 2012–2025 and places selected onshore flare locations in the context of nearby modelled population and mapped mangrove cover.

The main aim is evidence-led screening for abatement investigation and environmental-monitoring design. Priority classifications do not establish operational responsibility, actual emissions, measured exposure, environmental damage or the feasibility of gas-recovery projects.

04 / METHODS

Analytical approach

01

Inventory reconciliation

Reconcile annual flare estimates, stable site identities, coordinates and coastal/onshore assignment against validated administrative boundaries.

02

Historical activity

Summarize cumulative and recent 2023–2025 satellite-estimated volumes, positive-volume years and reported zeros. A reported zero is not proof of flare elimination.

03

Environmental context

Use GHSL 2020 residential population estimates within 5 km of each eligible site and ESA WorldCover 2021 mapped mangrove area within 2 km. These are distinct screening indicators.

04

Priority rules & sensitivity

Apply transparent, distribution-based thresholds and test whether candidate selections survive stricter screening assumptions.

05 / RESULTS

Which places need further checks

34 HIGH

Abatement investigation candidates with top-decile recent-volume evidence and at least 10 positive-volume years.

41 ELEVATED

Additional qualifying abatement candidates meeting the documented P75 recent-volume and persistence gates.

4 WATCHLIST

Top-decile recent-volume sites with shorter positive-volume histories; retained without changing their priority class.

24 COMMUNITY

Independent monitoring-screening candidates based on flaring evidence and nearby GHSL 2020 population context.

7 ECOSYSTEM

Independent monitoring-screening candidates based on flaring evidence and nearby WorldCover 2021 mapped mangrove context.

Population counts and mangrove areas are not combined into a single risk score. The ecosystem list is particularly sensitive to the chosen thresholds and should be treated as exploratory.

06 / INTERPRETATION

What the results cannot prove

  • Flare volume is a satellite-derived estimate, not a direct measurement of emissions, pollutant concentration or health outcomes.
  • The GHSL 2020 and WorldCover 2021 datasets provide contextual snapshots, not conditions contemporaneous with every annual flare observation through 2025.
  • Seven onshore sites contained GHSL -200 NoData raster values. These invalid cells were masked before the final population calculations.
  • Five-kilometre population buffers and two-kilometre mangrove buffers overlap. Their values must not be summed as unique people or unique habitat extent.
  • Mapped mangrove proximity does not establish pollution exposure, damage, causation or ecological condition.
  • No claims of operator liability, legal non-compliance or demonstrated gas-utilization feasibility are made.
