NIGER DELTA, NIGERIA · 2012–2025

Where should we look first to reduce gas flaring?

Gas flaring happens when natural gas is burned in a flame at oil and gas facilities. We studied satellite-based estimates to see where flaring has been reported, where it has been greatest, and which places deserve a closer look.

What did we do, and why?

You do not need a background in mapping or environmental science to use this atlas. Here is the idea in three short steps.

01

What we studied

We looked at 14 years (2012–2025) of satellite-based gas-flaring estimates from the World Bank. There are 476 locations in the Nigerian inventory. We examined 372 confirmed onshore locations in the Niger Delta more closely.

02

Why it matters

Burning gas wastes a resource and can raise environmental concerns. But not every location has the same history. We wanted to understand where flaring appears more substantial or persistent.

03

What we hope to achieve

Help researchers and decision-makers decide where to investigate reducing flaring and where further checks near communities or mangroves might be useful.

Important: The circles show recorded locations, not measured pollution. Nearby people or mangroves do not automatically mean they have been harmed. These are places to investigate, not confirmed problems.

HOW WE DID IT

How did we choose these places?

Which locations did we include?

World Bank GGFR individual flare-location estimates are joined to validated Nigeria ADM1 geometry. The 372 confirmed nine-state onshore sites form the analytical population. The 70 offshore, 33 ambiguous/conflicting and one other Nigerian onshore records are retained separately.

How did we check for nearby people and mangroves?

GHSL P2023A 2020 population_count was summed at native raster grid scale within individual 5 km geodesic buffers. Seven -200 NoData-affected pilot results were corrected before integration. ESA WorldCover v200 class 95 mapped mangrove area is pixel-area weighted in individual 2 km buffers for 2021.

What can this map NOT tell us?

It does not measure emissions, pollutant exposure, health effects, mangrove damage, regulatory responsibility or gas-utilization feasibility. It does not aggregate overlapping population or mangrove buffers.

Where did the information come from?
