THE RESEARCH

Separate evidence, separate decisions.

This atlas brings together satellite-derived flare-volume estimates, validated state assignment, recurrence evidence, and independent population and mangrove context. It does not turn these unlike quantities into a single score.

Use it to inspect the full inventory, understand the locked priority rules, and identify where verification, monitoring design, and feasibility investigation may be most useful.

Flare-evidence gate plus GHSL 2020 modelled population context at or above P75 in each individual 5 km buffer.

ECOSYSTEM CONTEXT

7 independent candidates

Flare-evidence gate plus ESA WorldCover 2021 class-95 mapped mangrove area at or above P75 in each individual 2 km buffer.

The shortlist is threshold-sensitive: a strict P90 context rule retains one site; strict flare-and-context selection retains none.

04 / METHODS & UNCERTAINTY

Reproducible screening, deliberately bounded claims.

Inventory and eligibility

World Bank GGFR individual flare-location estimates are joined to validated Nigeria ADM1 geometry. The 372 confirmed nine-state onshore sites form the analytical population. The 70 offshore, 33 ambiguous/conflicting and one other Nigerian onshore records are retained separately.

Environmental context

GHSL P2023A 2020 population_count was summed at native raster grid scale within individual 5 km geodesic buffers. Seven -200 NoData-affected pilot results were corrected before integration. ESA WorldCover v200 class 95 mapped mangrove area is pixel-area weighted in individual 2 km buffers for 2021.

What this atlas does not show

It does not measure emissions, pollutant exposure, health effects, mangrove damage, regulatory responsibility or gas-utilization feasibility. It does not aggregate overlapping population or mangrove buffers.
